## Player
- Classic shops, restaurants and fast food take customers in from the street again, about as many a day as the 1994 game served. A shopper who would find a full car's load already waiting at the elevator stays away, and one who waits 15 minutes walks back out, so they don't clog your elevators or sour your tenants' mood.
- A Classic shop now shows the shoppers inside it, and its card counts them.
